# Answer Key

## Planets Quiz
1. **b) Jupiter**: It is more than twice as massive as all the other planets combined.
2. **c) Mercury**: It orbits closest to the Sun.
3. **a) Mars**: Iron oxide (rust) in its soil gives it a red colour.
4. **c) Venus**: Its thick atmosphere traps heat, making it hotter than Mercury.
5. **b) Saturn**: Its rings are made of ice and rock.
